Healthcare sector

Specialists in healthcare facilities

Our experience

Professionalism in spaces where activity never stops

Our experience in hospitals, health centres and other healthcare facilities allows us to approach every project with professionalism, commitment and attention to detail.

We have worked in leading centres in Málaga and on the Costa del Sol, including the Hospital Regional Universitario de Málaga (Carlos Haya), the Hospital Civil de Málaga and the Hospital Costa del Sol in Marbella.

Our goal is to deliver quality solutions tailored to the needs of each facility and to the specific demands of the healthcare environment.

What we take into account in a healthcare centre

A hospital is not just any job site. Every intervention adapts to the centre's daily routine.

  • Respect for healthcare activityPatients, medical staff and the centre's schedules.
  • Technical coatings for each areaAntibacterial, epoxy and washable paints, depending on how each space is used.
  • Durable, easy-to-clean surfacesFinishes designed for hygiene and intensive use.
  • Order and cleanliness in every jobCare for the surroundings during and after the work.
